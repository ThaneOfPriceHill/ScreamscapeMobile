2027 - Bakunawa / “Project Purple” - (10/2/2026) Six Flags Great Adventure sent out a new video update showing off the Bakunawa ride cars being put together. There will be two trains, a purple and a green train, each five cars long.


 
    (7/30/2026) It looks like the rumors were right, because Six Flags Great Adventure confirmed this week that BAKUNAWA was coming in 2027. Just as described in the last update, the new coaster will be inspired by the Philippine legend of the BAKUNAWA sea serpent. Details about the ride experience and records it will break were also revealed.
    For starters, Bakunawa will be the world’s tallest and fastest spinning launch coaster, with the spiral tower at the end standing 382 feet tall, making it one of the five tallest roller coasters in the world. The top speed of the launched experience will hit 100mph, on what will be the world’s first free-spinning, floorless coaster trains, able to rotate freely throughout the ride experience in order to ensure that no two rides are exactly the same.
    In order to allow for better capacity, the station will be able to load two different trains, so one is ready to go when the other returns. According to the press release, the experience will last 2 minutes and 17 seconds, starting with the first of three magnetic launches that will propel the train from 0 to 60mph. As the train inverts upside-down into the world’s longest stall inversion, the trains will encounter the ride’s second launch while completely upside-down, that will boost the train from 60mph to 80mph. Rolling back upright, as the trains hit the bottom of the spiral tower they will run through the third launch zone that will accelerate the train up to 100mph as it blasts up the massive tower, into a spiral and into a 180º bank, revealing an “unprecedented” view of Six Flags Great Adventure below you… assuming you still have your eyes open at this point.
    “Each of the two, 20-passenger trains surfaces from the depths and speeds through the 3,163-foot course with its free-spinning, floorless vehicles rotating independently, creating a unique combination of spins, views and sensations every cycle.” Riders must be at least 48” tall to ride Bakunawa.
    All together, they say the coaster will take Six World Records:
World’s Fastest Spinning Coaster (100mph)
World’s Tallest Spinning Coaster (382 feet)
World’s Fastest Inversion (80mph)
World’s Longest Stall Inversion
World’s First Floorless Spinning Coaster
World’s First Upside-Down Launch


 
    (7/25/2026) The next and final teaser for Project Purple has been posted by Six Flags Great Adventure, which confirms that the big announcement will take place on July 28th.
    The funny thing is the true name of Project Purple has been the cause of so much debate this summer, which is great for the park, as everyone is talking about the next new coaster. Phantom Spire was a fun sounding name for sure, bug Gargantuan Vertical Vengeance is just too much of a mouthful and as a backup, I’ve been wondering if the park was trolling the enthusiast community with that one a little bit, especially by putting the GVV logo on the shipping container.
    One really fun theory has come out that I think has enough merit to be worth discussing, and that is the name Bakunawa. You haven’t seen this in a trademark filing most likely because it is a historic and public mythic character… not unlike other mythic characters like Thor, Zeus, Kraken and the like. I believe the way this works is that while a park can get a copyright on their particular version of a mythic character like this (such as Marvel’s Thor) the use for the name and concept itself are not exclusive, so that someone else could come up with their own version of it… much like how we’ve seen so many versions of Pinocchio or Hercules over the years, as the character itself become public domain.
    So you are likely asking yourself right now… just what the heck is a Bakunawa? It isn’t the most well known creature, but Bakunawa is a giant sea serpent / dragon like character from Philippine mythology who is also known as the “Moon-Eater”, and is such related to the cause of eclipses, earthquakes and some storms. As the legend goes, there were once seven moons in the sky and Bakunawa becamed obsessed with them and rose up from the sea and devoured six of them. You can see where this is going, as modern lunar eclipses are said to be Bakunawa attempting to swallow the final moon in the sky, and rituals would be performed making a lot of loud noise to drive Bakunawa back into the sea during the eclipse.
    While the announcement is set for July 28th… it is interesting to note that the last significant lunar eclipse to take place before New Year’s Eve 2028 is actually set for the evening a month later on August 27, 2026 for what is being called a “Deep Partial Lunar Eclipse” where 96% of the moon will slip into the Earth’s shadow. By now you will have also noticed that the Full Moon has been present in a lot of the Project Purple teaser material as well… while we’ve been all too focused on the shipping container itself, the full moon has been there in the background as well in much of it.
    So while we still don’t know the true name of Project Purple… we now have what could possibly be our first clue as to the theme of the ride.

In addition to some sea nets caught on the edges, and barnacles, there is an impression that makes it look like it may have been grabbed at one point by a giant tentacle. On the right side is another warning sticker of some kind… I can’t quite make out the first word, but it may say “BEACH WARNING” followed by a purple flag and the text, “Dangerous Sea Creatures”
    Oh… apparently a real life version of this crate has been dropped off a few days ago at the park by the entrance to Shoreline Pier, where a better look at this particular warning sign confirms it is a “Marine Life Hazzard”, along with a tag line at the bottom by a Shoreline Pier logo that says “Know the warning flags”.  And yes… fun fact… a Purple Flag is an official beach warning sign to indicate the presence of “Dangerous Marine Life”.
    So we’re clearly going for a sea creature / Kraken style motif it seems, and we won’t have long to wait, as it looks like the announcement is coming on July 28th.

    (3/19/2026) Six Flags Great Adventure has announced that they have begin vertical assembly of the park’s new for 2027 roller coaster. Even better, they sent some pictures of the monumental event as the first pieces are being set into place.

 
    (2/20/2026) The first piece of track for the Six Flags Great Adventure 2027 roller coaster has arrived… and it is very purple… and very much appears to be from Mack Rides. This also appears to be some kind of switch track, featuring what looks like locking pins only on the one side of the track. This isn’t a typical switch track, but is a long straight piece will a full set of LSM launch fins placed so that the direction of travel should be towards the pins.
    Based on this, I’m wondering if the ride will feature some kind of sideways sliding launch track… but without locking pins on both ends, it does make me wonder if we might be seeing something more akin to the load and sideways positioning tracks used on Six Flags’ Mr. Freeze coasters at Six Flags over Texas and St. Louis parks.
    I know it’s a bit much to assume from just pictures of a single track section, but this is a very unique looking piece of track, designed to serve a very specific purpose. I guess we’ll know more as as soon as Six Flags is ready to show off more of what they have planned.
    BUT… there are rumors about what this new project may be. It is still too early to tell, but some believe this may be a new Mack Rides coaster design concept that will be part of their Xtreme Spinning Coaster line of products, possibly to be known as an “Xtreme Spinning Tower Coaster”. The little we know about the early design concepts for this idea was that it would be a spinning shuttle coaster design that would launch up into an extended inverted roll-over section of track, and then spiral up and around a large vertical tower. So in a way this would be sort of replacing the lost Kingda Ka ride with another similar concept that would rise up and down a large vertical tower, just in a very different way.

???? - New Attraction Guest Survey Concepts - (10/18/2025) Six Flags has begun sending out surveys to select guests to get feedback about possible future addition concepts that may be in the works.
