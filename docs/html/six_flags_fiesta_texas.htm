    (5/31/2026) Werewolf Gorge is coming to Six Flags Fiesta Texas in 2027, as the longest launched family coaster in the world! The coaster, from Vekoma, is 4120 feet long and features four launch moments, 32 moments of floater air time and hits top speed of 45mph. From all appearances, this looks like they took inspiration from Dollywood's Big Bear Mountain and said make it even longer and add a few new tricks.
