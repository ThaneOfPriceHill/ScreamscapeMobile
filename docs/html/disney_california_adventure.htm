2028 - Avengers Campus Expansion / Avengers: Infinity Defense - (9/6/2026) Apparently the timeline for the new expansion of the Avengers Campus was established, and we won’t see the new attractions ready to come online until 2028. It’s kind of crazy as the Avengers Campus land itself was announced at D23 back in 2017… and then it took four years to opened the land in 2021, but it opened only with the Web Slingers attraction along with the front facade of the SHIELD building that was to serve as the entrance to an Avengers themed ride that was put on hold and sent back to get retooled as a Phase 2 expansion.

So much so that they had to hype it all up once again at D23 2026 last month… and it still won’t open for another two years.
    And I hate to say it, but if you look back at how Disney has opened a lot of big projects like this lately, they’ve ended up being multi-phase openings as well when more than one attraction is involved. You may recall Star Wars: Galaxy’s Edge opened ONLY with the Millennium Falcon’s Smuggler’s Run attraction in California in 2019 and did not finish the original vision that included the Rise of the Resistance attraction until early 2020. While we don’t have a timeline for Disney’s new land at Disney Animal Kingdom, I’ve been expecting to see the upcoming Encanto and Indiana Jones dark rides there open separately as well. So I won’t be surprised if the Stark Flight Lab attraction opens in early 2028, and Avengers Infinity Defense opens towards the middle or later part of the year separately.

    (8/9/2024) D23 is happening in California this week, and visuals of a ride-vehicle mock-up for the future Avengers dark ride coming to Avengers Campus was on display. For anyone who had doubts, you can see based on the design that this dark ride vehicle looks very similar to the dark rides you’ve probably see at Universal theme parks, such as the Adventures of Spider-Man or Transformers: The Ride.

    (9/9/2023) Work is moving forward on that long-promised Avengers attraction for the Avengers Campus in Disney California Adventure. According to a new piece of teaser artwork released, we get our first view of the “world jumping” ride vehicle that will transport guests throughout the Marvel Multiverse.
    While this may simply just be a piece of concept art showing the back-end of an Avengers themed flying vehicle, the mention that the ride experience will include traveling through the Multiverse does give us an important piece of information about the theme of the ride. The use of the Multiverse concept will easily allow access to all of the popular Marvel characters, no matter how far apart they are, including revisiting popular heroes who may have died (like Iron Man or Blank Panther) as well as “variant” characters that may currently only exist in other parallel universes (like Captain Carter, Deadpool or the Spider-Man variants).
    The concept is also a brilliant one that could be used to update the attraction moving forward, as future Marvel productions introduce all new characters in the years to come, without breaking the overall theme of the ride. Much like what we have with the current version of Star Tours, all characters from any point Marvel history will be fair game to mix and merge for use in a wild romp through Marvel’s vast Mutliverse.
    As for the ride itself... little is known. A previous Avengers themed concept also apparently involved a bit of teleporting around the Multiverse to defeat a threat known as King Thanos, from an alternerate universe where Thanos defeated the Avengers and won. This attraction was thought to be something of a hybrid between Disney’s Rise of the Resistance dark ride but with more multi-media and projections to convey the action, not unlike Universal’s Transformers dark ride. Based on the look of the vehicle shown in the concept art, we’ve moved on from a “ground” based vehicle to a themed flying vehicle with tall sidepanels, which looks very similar in nature to the layout of the new Peter Pan ride coming to Tokyo DisneySea next year. If you’ve been on Universal’s Transformers or Universal’s Spider-Man dark rides, then you’ll remember that they also feature high side panels to help focus the rider’s view on what is happening in in the scene or screen front of them.

???? - Avatar Attraction  - (8/23/2026) While Disney hasn’t confirmed anything regarding the ride system for the proposed Avatar boat ride attraction for California Adventure, a new post from ThemeParkInsider does share some of my own thoughts on the project.     (4/18/2026) Could the previously planned Avatar land at the Disneyland Resort be replaced by something else? This rumor has been tossed around ever since former Imagineer Jim Shull posted a thought on social media awhile back, that pitched the idea that adding a Zootopia themed land and attraction like the one added to Shanghai Disneyland, might be a better fit at the Disney California Adventure park than the Avatar-land.
    The basis was that while the last Avatar film did good box office, it maybe wasn’t the runaway hit that the previous two films were, and cited that the general public may have hit the point of suffering from a bit of Avatar exhaustion. I can see that… the point is valid… and if you have seen the Pandora theme land they built at Disney Animal Kingdom in Florida, as impressive as it is… it requires a massive amount of space to pull off the visual illusions to fully immerse the guests. This is land that, quite frankly, they don’t really have available in California… and certainly not in the tiny corner of the park where they said they would bring Avatar to replace the current Monsters Inc dark ride.
    The ongoing argument for Zootopia is that the second film that just hit the theaters over the holiday season did way better box-office that they anticipated, and the existing attraction they built in China is also highly regarded. If there is a flaw to this argument, it’s that the Zootopia ride itself is really sort of a copy of the Star Wars: Rise of the Resistance attraction, using the same style ride hardware and a very similar layout concept… but with an experience that has been totally reimagined to fit the Zootopia universe and with a storyline that fits will with the two films. The more compact and urban landscape of the themed land would also be a better fit for this section of the California Adventure park which will essentially be packed in where the Hollywood area transitions into the Marvel themed Avengers Campus.
    Another big piece of the rumor is that originally Disney announced that the current Monsters Inc ride would be closed for good in early 2026 to make way for Avatar… and now they’ve delayed this closure until 2027. So why did they need the extra year? Possibly to work on a new concept… either to revise and improve the Avatar plans, or perhaps to work on pivoting to an entire new plan for the area… like Zootopia… or perhaps something else entirely like a copy of the Muppet’s themed Rock ‘n Roller Coaster perhaps if it proves to be a hit. (After all… the Muppets did once inhabit this area of the park once-upon-a-time with a copy of the MuppetVision attraction that closed back in 2014 to make way for Mickey’s PhilharMagic.
    Historically, this corner of DCA has always been a bit of a problem for the company, so when they finally start building something new to go here, they’ll want to get it right.

    (6/6/2025) After having some time to think about it, I’m going to make a few predictions and speculation about the new “Avatar Experience” area now being planned for Disney California Adventure. Much of the artwork Disney has released thus far seems to indicate that not only will this new land not be a copy of the “Pandora - The World of Avatar” land built in Walt Disney World’s Animal Kingdom, but that also doesn’t appear that they will even try to copy that park’s popular “Flight of Passage” attraction at all.
    In a way, while the first Avatar themed land was inspired by the lush jungle scenery from the first Avatar film, this second land appears to instead be inspired by the oceanfront location used for the sequel, Avatar: The Way of Water. Many of the artwork pieces revealed so far for the California version showcase the iconic rocky structure known as The Cove of the Ancestors in the background.  The other item clear from the artwork is that the ride system to be used here will be a boat / flume ride of some kind that may feature an outdoor section in addition to the  more traditional dark ride indoor space.
    The first thought that also comes to my mind is that this could possibly be the first use outside of China of the revolutionary new ride system WDI created for Pirates of the Caribbean at Shanghai Disneyland. While on the surface, this ride system appears similar to the more traditional boat ride used for the other Pirates rides, the new system allows for more controlled movements of the boats through the various scenes, including speed as well as the ability to actually rotation the boats to direct the gaze of the riders at the various scenes, both real and projection. Adding more fuel to the fire is the fact that the Shanghai Disneyland version of Pirates fits into a fairly compact space compared to the much bigger show buildings used for the American versions. Based on aerial images, it does look like the Shanghai show building would actually fit quite well into the north-eastern corner of California Adventure, replacing both the Monsters Inc. dark ride as well as the former Who Wants To Be a Millionaire soundstage structure on the North side of the monorail beam.
    Another thing to remember is that it appears that the project will actually have a little more space to work with as well. If you look at the artwork Disney released this week showing off the new elevated pathway that will lead to the parks from the new parking structure they want to build across Harbor Blvd. from the parks. Notice that they have entirely removed the large space currently used as a bus and tram stop here, keeping the guest pathway on the far northern edge of the space only near the northern monorail beam. A new walkway will instead go under here and move guests towards the Harbor Blvd. area where they confirmed that they will build a new “transportation hub” to replace the old one. While not shown, the flow of guests from the park will instead move south down Harbor, where the bus stops will likely replace area currently used as a passenger drop off lot, and keep going south as needed to allow better access in handout via the existing traffic light at the intersection of Harbor & Disney Way. Disney Way is likely where most of the car traffic coming off I-5 Northbound will also be directed when leading cars into the new parking structure on this side of the park, with Southern-bound traffic directed into the existing Mickey & Friends structure.
