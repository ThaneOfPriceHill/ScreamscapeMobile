2027 - Monsteropolis - (10/10/2026) Disney has posted a few more in-depth details about the food and beverage options coming to the Monsteropolis land when it opens at Disney’s Hollywood Studios in 2027. The land will feature two main food and beverage locations… a recreation of Harryhausen’s Restaurant as seen in the first animated film along with Archie’s Scare Pig Pizza.
    Harryhausen’s will be a table service restaurant that will offer sushi along with ramen based dishes, and a spattering of Japanese inspired flavors all given a little Monsters style twist. Archie’s will be the quick-serve Pizza eatery that is obviously serving as a replacement for the lost PizzeRizzo. Archie’s will be themed as a college hang-out as inspired by the characters from Monsters University ad feature “Blort”, a drink from the film that will be a frozen-purple beverage topped with “Scream Apple Foam”.

    (6/11/2026) It was first spotted in the concept artwork for the new Monsters Inc themed city area, but now Disney Imagineering has also confirmed that there will be an official Harryhausen’s restaurant for guests to dine-in, just as seen in the movie.
 
    (6/6/2026) Disney Imagineering has dropped new details about the upcoming Monsters Inc. themed land replacing the former Muppets area at Disney’s Hollywood Studios. Construction has been ongoing for months now, with most of the fan focus being on the location of the future Monsters themed Door Coaster.
    This time Disney choose to put some focus on what they had planned as the build-out of the Monteropolis city area, where guests will enter as part of a new “H.U.M.A.N. Day” holiday experience, which stands for “Humans Understanding Monsters Are Nice”. As you may recall from the films, the arrival of any human into the monster world was cause for much panic and chaos, so this is a new era where the two worlds are starting out an attempt at the creation of a new growing relationship built on understanding and not fear.
    Also, Disney Imagineers cited inspiration for Monsteropolis from a previous Disney project in Shanghai, claiming that they wanted to build Monsteropolis to have the same level of detail and lived in feeling that they were able to successfully create with the Zootopia land at Shanghai Disneyland. They’ve been working with Pixar closely, including the director of the original film, in order to create this living world of Monsteropolis.

    (3/9/2025) Disney has released an interesting technical animation video clip showcasing what the loading station of the new Monsters Inc. inverted roller coaster will be like. In the very brief animation you can see that the trains appear to arrive on the Screen Floor on two side-by-side tracks, but the seats themselves are turned 90º to the side to allow for riders to load in a continuously row on either side of the station. Once loaded, what at first appears to be a single long row of seats will rise vertically up to the roof, not unlike one of the famous Doors from the Scream Floor, and as the train rolls out of site, seats will rotate into the forward facing position in pairs to take on the look of a more standard inverted coaster train.
    If  you look carefully in the background of the image, you can also see that there is another pair of stations on the other side of the building, which will also rise up separately onto their own tracks along the roof of that side of the building as well, sending the trains into the Door Vault.

 
    (8/14/2024) An interesting looking model of the proposed Monsters Inc. Door Coaster was on display at D23, which you can see in the video below. Disney mentioned that they wanted guests to experience what it was like when those robot-arms would grab and lift the doors from the Scare Floor to the ceiling track and then zoom off to the storage area, and it looks like the station has been designed to do just that based on the look of the model.
    The load area appears to divide into two load/unload bays, each of which will have a left and right track position, so I’m guessing whatever elevator style lift they are planning will send one track up when the other goes down. Once at the top, the tracks will all merge into one and then appears to head on over towards the larger door storage building where the majority of the ride will be enclosed, entering from the outside into the top of the storage bay through a hole in the upper right corner, not unlike how Tron Lightcycle/Run does.
    Other than the vertical lift out of the station, the layout does not appear to feature any kind of traditional lift hill system, though there are some longer straight track sections that could potentially hold boosters to help the train maintain speed if necessary, as well as serve as a block/brake location in the event of an E-Stop.

 
    (8/11/2024) A new ride concept that has been kicking around since 2001 was just confirmed at D23 last night. Anyone remember the rumored Monsters Inc. Door Coaster concept for Disney’s Hollywood Studios?
    This time it isn’t just a new ride, but a whole Monsters Inc. themed mini-land where you can stroll the streets of Monsteropolis and go on a tour of the power plant and get to ride on a suspended coaster through the door vault as seen in the classic film finale.
    According to the article, work will begin on the project sometime next year (2025), so I guess the next question is to wonder where exactly in the park this will go?  Given the proximity to the Pixar Place and Toy Story Land, I do wonder if it’s time to wipe out the old Animation Courtyard building complex? Of course this could also mark the end of the line of the Muppets Courtyard area too if they wanted to build it on the other side of the park.
    I guess we’ll have to wait and see. As for the ride itself, based on the concept artwork this appears to be a Vekoma style family inverted coaster, which fits in with the rumors for the past 20+ years.
