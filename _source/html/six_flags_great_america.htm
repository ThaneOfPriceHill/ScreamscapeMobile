Park News - (10/19/2026) In a curious move, a reader has reported that the test site that normally sat outside of the Raging Bull coaster at Six Flags Great America (also a B&M Hypercoaster) has been quietly removed. Our source claims to have been at the park on Monday when the incident took place at the Georgia park on Goliath and saw the Raging Bull test seat then, but upon returning to the park over this weekend, they noticed it had strangely been removed.

 
    (5/30/2026) Huge news from Six Flags Great America as the park has confirmed that for the first time in years the legendary dual-track wooden coaster, American Eagle, will resume true side-by-side racing operations this season! They say that the iconic 1981 wooden coaster hasn’t regularly raced its trains in nearly 10 years. Returning Park President, John Krajnak, noted that bringing back the racing aspect is a primary way they are leaning into pure nostalgia for the park's milestone 50th Anniversary celebration.
    Well done Six Flags, so good to see this happening!

 

2027 - Camp Timber Trail / New Kids Area - (8/8/2026) A quick update on the new on the Camp Timber Trail coming to Six Flags Great America next season. The “Trail Trotters” attraction isn’t a walk-through as I thought, but really a small ride for kids where they can ride on the back of various critter themed vehicles along a short track. The general term for these style rides is often referred to as a Pony Trek ride, as many ofter a simple horse / pony ride theme, though Dollywood has Black Bear Trail where kids ride on small bears and many Legoland parks have versions with a Knight Jousting theme.
    The nearby Forest Grove Playpark is the outdoor nature themed play space.

 
    (8/6/2026) Six Flags Great America has announced Camp Timber Trail is coming in 2027. The new family area will feature 3 NEW attractions, 4 reimagined rides, and 2 returning favorites. And no… no PEANUTS or Snoopy here… though it does very much have a very similar look to a Camp Snoopy area… just more of a what if you built Camp Snoopy without Snoopy.  So keep that in mind as Six Flags is still figuring out how to handle their PEANUTS vs Looney Tunes IP management. Ok, back to Camp Timber Trail!
    The big new coaster coming in 2027 here will be called Sky Hawk, which will be the mid-west’s longest, tallest and fastest suspended family coaster. The other two brand new attractions are: Trail Trotters and Forest Grove Play Park. Both seem to be themed play spaces for the smaller children… so sorry… no more new rides.
    The reimagined rides coming to the land include: Chirp Chaser (formerly the Sprocket Rockets coaster), Wildness Wagon (formerly Crazy Bus), Creek Cruiser (formerly Yahoo River) and BeeBoppin (formerly Buzzy Beez). The Logger’s Run flume ride and Little Dipper family coaster will remain where they are, but become part of this new land.

    (8/3/2026) According to a new rumor, there is a chance that the new kids area coming to Six Flags Great America in 2027 just might be Snoopy / Peanuts themed. The Cedar Fair parks were known for having the exclusive use of the Snoopy / Peanuts IP across North America for their theme parks while Six Flags was more focused on the Looney Tunes characters for their parks. Ever since the merger, there has been a bit of a debate on if the merged chain should perhaps focus on just one IP over the other, rather than keep both, but the question no one has been able to answer yet is which IP might prove to be more popular. Certainly, adding Snoopy and pals to a major Six Flags theme park would be a great way to test out which IP seems to perform better with park guests.
    Now… one of our sources have pointed out that Six Flags may have been sending out some hints of their own about Snoopy’s potential arrival at the park. A new teaser for #TreeTopAdventure27 on social media posted shows a couple following a map in the park to the Yukon Territory, followed by another bracelet that clearly says “Aug. Sixth”, so it looks like we have an announcement date now set for Thursday. However… there may be another much more hidden clue buried in this video… the background banjo music being played is thought to be the same song used on a PEANUTS clip of Snoopy and his siblings playing in a band. Another very hidden item is if you look closely at the man’s left arm (holding the map), he’s got a green bandanna tied on by the elbow. You can barely see it, but our source believes it looks similar to an item for sale that features Snoopy on it, but to be honest, that may be a bit of a stretch.
    There is the inclusion of the Yukon Territory sign as well, which could likely mean that the entire land will be transformed into whatever this new theme is, which could also mean that the Logger’s Run flume ride could be given a makeover as well. Kings Island did this with the park’s old Log Ride by rebranding it as Charlie Brown’s Rushing River Log Ride during that park’s Camp Snoopy expansion. Previously it had also been known as Race for Your Life Charlie Brown, named after the title of a Peanuts animated special where the characters go on a wild river raft race.

???? - New Attraction Guest Survey Concepts - (10/18/2025) Six Flags has begun sending out surveys to select guests to get feedback about possible future addition concepts that may be in the works. Keep in mind that not every concept they pitch for any given park may actually be intended for that particular park, as sometimes the chains will pitch ride concepts to other markets as well to gain some broader feedback on certain concepts. With that said… let’s dig into what is currently being pitched for the various parks.
    The survey sent out to Six Flags Great America guests has suggested ideas such as a new “Family Thrill Launch Coaster” suitable for the younger guests as well as the whole family that would include some “themed storytelling, immersive elements” and more. So this isn’t a kiddie ride, and if they tap Vekoma for something like this, then anything remotely like Big Bear Mountain that was added to Dollywood last year would be a big winner at just about any park they would want to bring it to.
    Another concept pitched for Six Flags Great America would have likely been right at home inside the park’s former IMAX / Pictorum theater building which was demolished to make way for Maxx Force a few years ago. So it seems Six Flags is asking guests how they feel about adding a Flying Theater to the park. This may also be due to the popularity of the great “Flyover” Flying Theater ride that was just added to Chicago’s Navy Pier in 2024.
    While the first two concepts are clearly trying to find a way to bring more families to the park, the last concept is for thrill seekers, as they propose a Giga Coaster concept.
