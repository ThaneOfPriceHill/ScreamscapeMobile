Park News - (9/2/2026) As we reported late last month, Six Flags Darien Lake has now confirmed that they will not be hosting a “Fright Fest” Halloween style night event this year. Instead they will shift their focus towards a more family friendly day-time event celebrations instead. This includes adding four weekends of Oktoberfest (now running every weekend in September) and then Kids Boo Fest will take place Sept. 19-20 and Sept. 26-27th.
    For those that frequent the park, this can’t come as too much of a surprise, due to the rise in security related events over the past few years during after dark special events or concerts.
    (8/24/2026) I’m not sure if this is a new change or something that’s been established for the summer, but there does not appear to be any plans to host a Fright Fest Halloween style event at Six Flags Darien Lake this fall. The park will simply go to weekends only operations in September and even those will end with a final operating day slated for Sunday, October 11th.

 
    (8/23/2026) Theme Park Cubbies… you know, those little boxes where riders will stash away their loose objects while taking a spin on the roller coaster? They all essentially work on a honor system and you have to hope that no one grabs your stuff while you’re out on the ride, and typically your stuff is usually there, ready and waiting for you upon your return. That’s good for a plush bear you won, your refillable drink cup, or easily replaceable personal items, however you probably aren’t going to throw a purse, wallet or your cell phone in them… just in case, right?
    In a bit of a weird story, a man visited Six Flags Darien Lake this summer and while riding the park’s Tantrum roller coaster, he was instructed to remove his sandals and place them in a cubby while on the ride. Upon returning… the sandals were gone, and he is now filing a lawsuit against the park for mishandling his property, and not providing any form of temporary aid or footwear to allow him to walk across the park’s hot summer pathways to speak with security and management about the issue. During his walk across the hot pavement he suffered burns to his bare feet that also needed to be treated at the park.
    I feel for the guy… I do… there is nothing funny about burning your bare feet on hot pavement, but at the same time we all know you take a risk when putting anything in those cubbies.
    On and a personal note (or rant?)… I really don’t get why anyone wears loose fitting sandals or, the horror or all horrors… crocs, to theme parks when you damn well know you’re going to have to take them off several times during the day when jumping on various rides. Seriously… if you’re riding a B&M Flyer… no one seated behind you wants to be forced to stare are your dirty ratchet bare feet anyway and I’ve lost track of the times I’ve ridden on Soarin’ at Disney and ended up being distracted by bare feet from the row above me dangling down into my field of view. So for the sake of everyone, wear some decent footwear to the theme park the next time you visit. Your feet will thank you… as well as your fellow riders.
