General Park News - (8/12/2026) While we don’t know the extent of the changes yet, it seems Tokyo DisneySea has announced that the park’s Indiana Jones dark ride will be ready to reopen to guests on November 30th.

 
    (3/10/2026) One of the next big projects on deck for Tokyo DisneySea is a refurbishment / upgrade to the park’s Port Discovery land, which will also call for the removal of the park’s beloved Aquatopia ride. An exact closing date has not been announced yet, but many expect that it may close as early as late 2026.

 

2026 - 25th Sparkling Jubilee Celebration - (8/31/2025) Tokyo DisneySea will celebrate the park’s 25th anniversary from April 15, 2026 through to March 31, 2027 with a “Sparkling Jubilee” celebration. The event will feature new entertainment, special new Jubilee Blue park decor, special food and beverage additions and of course, limited time 25th anniversary merchandise. This will include all new Duffy and Friends merchandise, as the character first debuted as DisneySea 20 years ago.
    Meanwhile look for Mickey and friends to present a whole new show on a special barge in the Mediterranean Harbor called “Sparkling Jubilee Celebration”, along with a musical dance show in the American Waterfront called “Dance the Globe”.
